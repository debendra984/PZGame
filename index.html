About PuzzleMaster

PuzzleMaster is a multiplayer puzzle-adventure game built in Unreal Engine 4.27, powered by Epic Online Services for player authentication, friends list, presence, lobbies, and peer-to-peer multiplayer sessions.

Currently in active development. Designed for general audiences.

Privacy Policy

We respect user privacy. PuzzleMaster collects only the data required by Epic Online Services to enable:

  • Account login through Epic Account
  • Friends list display
  • Online presence indication
  • Multiplayer session matchmaking

We do not sell, share, rent, or transfer any user data to third parties. All data processing is handled by Epic Games under their Privacy Policy. Users may request data deletion by contacting the email below.
