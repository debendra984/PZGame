Our Product

PuzzleMaster is a multiplayer game built in Unreal Engine 4.27 using Epic Online Services for player authentication, friends list, presence, lobbies, and peer-to-peer sessions. The game is currently in development and is intended for general audiences (rating equivalent: PEGI 7 / ESRB Everyone).

Features

  • Online multiplayer lobbies
  • Friends list and player presence
  • Cross-platform compatibility
