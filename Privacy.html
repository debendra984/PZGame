Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Information We Collect

PuzzleMaster collects only the data required by Epic Online Services to enable core game features:

  • Epic Account ID for player authentication
  • Friends list (with user consent)
  • Online presence indicator
  • Multiplayer session and lobby data

2. How We Use Information

Collected data is used solely to provide multiplayer functionality, display friends/presence, and match players together in sessions. We do not use data for marketing, advertising, or analytics beyond Epic's standard SDK telemetry.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, rent, or transfer any user data to third parties. All data processing is handled by Epic Games under their Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Users may request deletion of their data by contacting us at the email below. We will process the deletion request within 30 days. Account data managed by Epic Games is governed by Epic's own privacy policy.

5. Children's Privacy

PuzzleMaster is rated PEGI 7 / ESRB Everyone. We comply with applicable children's privacy laws including COPPA (United States) and DPDP Act (India). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children below the minimum age set by Epic Games.
